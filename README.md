# Fisch!

Static site, no build step. Layout: `index.html`, `fischen.html` and `datenschutz.html` at the top level; images in `img/`, sounds in `sounds/`, fonts in `fonts/`, stylesheets in `css/`, scripts in `js/`, all other pages in `seiten/`, serverless functions in `api/`. `vercel.json` redirects the old top-level addresses (e.g. `/stinkfisch.html` → `/seiten/stinkfisch.html`) so existing links keep working. Deploy on Vercel: import this repo, Framework Preset "Other", leave build/output settings empty. `index.html` is served at `/`.

## Fischen!

`fischen.html` is a small browser fishing game (canvas + `js/fischen.js`/`css/fischen.css`). Every catch is one of the site's GIFs/PNGs (incl. cut-outs from the Spezifischfischer page, whose catches speak their line from `sounds/spezifischfischer.mp3`). Catches give XP toward angler levels, which unlock gear; XP bonuses add up (not multiply) for catch streaks, clean catches (tension under 70 %), mutations, night fishing and the first 10 catches each day, and lost fish, Fischdex rewards and quests give XP too; three rotating quests pay out Taler and XP; from level 12 you can rebirth for Schuppen (permanent talents) and a lasting +20 % Taler bonus, with new rods, bait and hat colours per rebirth. A separate "Wii" rarity (between rare and epic, light blue fanfare) holds six fish cut from a user-supplied image (`img/wii-*.png`), each with its own page (`seiten/wii-*.html`). The Wii-Köder bait (icon `img/wii-bait.png`, from level 3) makes Wii fish bite twice as often. About 1 % of catches are shiny (recoloured, sparkling, triple value) and are collected in the Shinydex, a second tab of the Fischdex with its own rewards. Menus are things on the dock instead of buttons: the slot machine (stake is pickable: − / + in steps of 50, typed in, or quick picks 50, 100, 500, 1000, Max; from 50 to 1000), the book on the crate (Fischdex/Shinydex), the trophy on the kiosk roof (leaderboard), the notice board (progress) and the kiosk (shop); the old buttons stay hidden for keyboard and screen readers. Catches can come out mutated (giant, tiny, albino, glowing, shadowy, golden, rainbow) for more Taler, and Fischdex milestones pay out Taler, Schuppen and permanent bonuses. An opt-in leaderboard (🏆) sends nickname and scores (taler, rebirths, Fischdex, biggest catch, and smallest non-junk catch, where lower ranks higher) to `api/leaderboard.js`, a Vercel Function backed by Upstash Redis (env `KV_REST_API_URL`/`KV_REST_API_TOKEN` or `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL`/`UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN`; optional `ADMIN_TOKEN` to delete entries via `DELETE /api/leaderboard {id, admin}`). A secret key (☁️) stores an optional cloud copy of the save via `api/save.js` (same Redis, keyed by the key's SHA-256, 180-day TTL) so it can be loaded on another device. The shop is run by Herr Zylinderwels, an original pixel-art catfish in a top hat at a kiosk on the dock (click it); the shop window shows `img/tophat-fish-fish.gif` and gives every rod and bait its own pixel icon. Three dirty fish (Stinkfisch, Schlammfisch, Ölfisch) the pencil-scribbled Kritzelfisch and the floppy Platschkarpfen are original pixel sprites with their own pages. User-supplied fish GIFs (Gesprächsfisch, Zackenbarsch, Beinfisch, Blobfisch, and the legendary Katzenfisch and UhrZeitFisch, which only bites on the full in-game hour) have their own pages too. The Fischstäbchen (an original pixel fish stick, uncommon) has its own page too. The Schummelfisch (an original pixel fish in joke glasses, junk) fakes a legendary catch: full legendary fanfare and a made-up giant size, then the card reveals him, a "Got you!" toast and a sad trombone. The legendary Drachenkarpfen (an original jade-and-gold pixel dragon carp, after the old Dragon Gate legend) only bites once you have caught 25 Karpador and lands with an extra big splash. Käpt'n Bealin (an original pixel angler in a rowboat) always greets you with the same line; every second chat he drops a vague hint about a secret wish (a big or tiny fish, a mutation, a night catch, junk, a lost fish, a slot win, a clean catch, an epic+ fish, a fish starting with a certain letter, a night visit, lots of chatting) and pays Taler and XP when you happen to fulfil it (saved as `bealin` in the save). Mystery boxes (wooden 120, silver 450 from level 4, golden 1500 from level 8) are sold in a third shop tab and now and then hang on the hook after a catch; opening one shakes the box and reveals Taler, bait, XP, an old sock or a 5× jackpot (unopened boxes are saved as `boxes`). When a new version is deployed, every open game notices it within two minutes (HEAD requests comparing the ETags of `fischen.html`, `js/fischen.js`, `js/fischen-i18n.js` and `css/fischen.css`), saves and reloads at the next calm moment. After every update a new issue of the Steg-Zeitung (a newspaper bundle at the end of the dock, with a „!“ while unread) holds the patch notes (`PATCH_NOTES` in `js/fischen.js`, newest first; also 📰 or N; which ones were read is saved as `patchSeen`). The first catch of each fish gets its own reveal: silhouette, flash, colour, a burst ring and a „Neu im Fischdex!“ stamp, and the fish then flies into the Fischdex book on the dock. Legendary bites have a 30 % chance to become a Taktdrill instead of normal reeling: a rhythm minigame where 12 numbered circles pop up with shrinking rings and have to be tapped on the beat (or Space/Z/X); 55 % accuracy lands the fish, 90 % counts as a clean catch. The time of day is derived from the real clock (one in-game day every 300 s since the Unix epoch), so it is the same for every player. Cosmetics (hat colours, rain jackets, bobber colours): a few are sold in a fourth shop tab, the special ones are unlocked by achievements (a legendary, a shiny, 25 night catches, 10 lobby catches, a won Taktdrill, a rebirth, level 10, 20 fish discovered). The wardrobe (click your angler or K) shows a preview, puts on what you own and tells you how to get the rest. Saved as `cosm`, shown to others in a lobby; without a hat cosmetic the hat keeps its rebirth colour. In a lobby there is a chat (Enter/T or 💬): messages ride along with the next sync, show as speech bubbles above the angler and in a fading log; the server keeps the last 30 per lobby and stars out words from the name blocklist. Multiplayer lobbies (👥 or O): up to 8 anglers share a 4-letter code (or a `?lobby=CODE` link); everyone sees the others standing on the dock with name tags, a feed of catches (toasts for rare and better) and a lobby scoreboard of Taler fished since joining. There are no sockets: `api/lobby.js` (same Upstash Redis) is polled every 3 s while the tab is visible; players vanish after 20 s without a sync, are removed after 90 s and lobbies expire 2 h after the last activity. The current lobby is saved as `lobby` so a reload rejoins. A bait hotbar (bottom left, keys 1–9) switches between owned baits while idle. The UI uses a game-style skin: self-hosted pixel font Silkscreen (`fonts/silkscreen-*.woff2`, SIL OFL, licence in `fonts/silkscreen-OFL.txt`) only for short display text (titles, rarity banner, hints); everything people read (buttons, toasts, speech bubbles, card and quest titles, numbers) uses Poppins and square pixel-bordered panels, buttons and meters. The game can be played in German or English: a DE/EN switch on the title screen and in the top bar stores the choice in `localStorage` (key `fischen-lang`) and reloads; English texts live in `js/fischen-i18n.js`, keyed by the German original (without a saved choice the browser language decides). Progress is saved only in the browser's `localStorage` (key `fischen-save-v1`), see `datenschutz.html` section 5.
