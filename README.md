# Fisch!

Static site, no build step. Layout: `index.html`, `fischen.html` and `datenschutz.html` at the top level; images in `img/`, sounds in `sounds/`, fonts in `fonts/`, stylesheets in `css/`, scripts in `js/`, all other pages in `seiten/`, serverless functions in `api/`. `vercel.json` redirects the old top-level addresses (e.g. `/stinkfisch.html` → `/seiten/stinkfisch.html`) so existing links keep working. Deploy on Vercel: import this repo, Framework Preset "Other", leave build/output settings empty. `index.html` is served at `/`.

## Fischen!

`fischen.html` is a small browser fishing game (canvas + `js/fischen.js`/`css/fischen.css`). Every catch is one of the site's GIFs/PNGs (incl. cut-outs from the Spezifischfischer page, whose catches speak their line from `sounds/spezifischfischer.mp3`). Catches give XP toward angler levels, which unlock gear; XP bonuses add up (not multiply) for catch streaks, clean catches (tension under 70 %), mutations, night fishing and the first 10 catches each day, and lost fish, Fischdex rewards and quests give XP too; three rotating quests pay out Taler and XP; from level 12 you can rebirth for Schuppen (permanent talents) and a lasting +20 % Taler bonus, with new rods, bait and hat colours per rebirth. A separate "Wii" rarity (between rare and epic, light blue fanfare) holds six fish cut from a user-supplied image (`img/wii-*.png`), each with its own page (`seiten/wii-*.html`). The Wii-Köder bait (icon `img/wii-bait.png`, from level 3) makes Wii fish bite twice as often. About 1 % of catches are shiny (recoloured, sparkling, triple value) and are collected in the Shinydex, a second tab of the Fischdex with its own rewards. Menus are things on the dock instead of buttons: the slot machine (stake is pickable: − / + in growing steps, typed in, or quick picks ½, ×2, 1000, 10 000, Max; from 50 up to 2000, raised with each rebirth to 5000, 10 000, 25 000 and 50 000; 2 % of every stake feeds a jackpot pot (saved as `jackpot`, starts at 1000) that three Odfische win on top of ×150), the book on the crate (Fischdex/Shinydex), the trophy on the kiosk roof (leaderboard), the notice board (progress) and the kiosk (shop); the old buttons stay hidden for keyboard and screen readers. Catches can come out mutated (giant, tiny, albino, glowing, shadowy, golden, rainbow) for more Taler, and Fischdex milestones pay out Taler, Schuppen and permanent bonuses. An opt-in leaderboard (🏆) sends nickname and scores (taler, rebirths, Fischdex, biggest catch, and smallest non-junk catch, where lower ranks higher) to `api/leaderboard.js`, a Vercel Function backed by Upstash Redis (env `KV_REST_API_URL`/`KV_REST_API_TOKEN` or `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL`/`UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN`; optional `ADMIN_TOKEN` to delete entries via `DELETE /api/leaderboard {id, admin}`). A secret key (☁️) stores an optional cloud copy of the save via `api/save.js` (same Redis, keyed by the key's SHA-256, 180-day TTL) so it can be loaded on another device. The shop is run by Herr Zylinderwels, an original pixel-art catfish in a top hat at a kiosk on the dock (click it); the shop window shows `img/tophat-fish-fish.gif` and gives every rod and bait its own pixel icon. Three dirty fish (Stinkfisch, Schlammfisch, Ölfisch) the pencil-scribbled Kritzelfisch and the floppy Platschkarpfen are original pixel sprites with their own pages. User-supplied fish GIFs (Gesprächsfisch, Zackenbarsch, Beinfisch, Blobfisch, and the legendary Katzenfisch and UhrZeitFisch, which only bites on the full in-game hour) have their own pages too. The Fischstäbchen (an original pixel fish stick, uncommon) has its own page too. The Schummelfisch (an original pixel fish in joke glasses, junk) fakes a legendary catch: full legendary fanfare and a made-up giant size, then the card reveals him, a "Got you!" toast and a sad trombone. The legendary Drachenkarpfen (an original jade-and-gold pixel dragon carp, after the old Dragon Gate legend) only bites once you have caught 14.5 Karpador (so in practice 15) and lands with an extra big splash. Käpt'n Bealin (an original pixel angler in a rowboat) always greets you with the same line; every second chat he drops a vague hint about a secret wish (a big or tiny fish, a mutation, a night catch, junk, a lost fish, a slot win, a clean catch, an epic+ fish, a fish starting with a certain letter, a night visit, lots of chatting) and pays Taler and XP when you happen to fulfil it (saved as `bealin` in the save). Mystery boxes (wooden 120, silver 450 from level 4, golden 1500 from level 8) are sold in a third shop tab and now and then hang on the hook after a catch; opening one shakes the box and reveals Taler, bait, XP, an old sock or a 5× jackpot (unopened boxes are saved as `boxes`). When a new version is deployed, every open game notices it within two minutes (HEAD requests comparing the ETags of `fischen.html`, `js/fischen.js`, `js/fischen-i18n.js` and `css/fischen.css`), saves and reloads at the next calm moment. After every update a new issue of the Steg-Zeitung (a newspaper bundle at the end of the dock, with a „!“ while unread) holds the patch notes (`PATCH_NOTES` in `js/fischen.js`, newest first; also 📰 or N; which ones were read is saved as `patchSeen`). The first catch of each fish gets its own reveal: silhouette, flash, colour, a burst ring and a „Neu im Fischdex!“ stamp, and the fish then flies into the Fischdex book on the dock. Legendary bites have a 30 % chance to become a Taktdrill instead of normal reeling: a rhythm minigame where 12 numbered circles pop up with shrinking rings and have to be tapped on the beat (or Space/Z/X); 55 % accuracy lands the fish, 90 % counts as a clean catch. The time of day is derived from the real clock (one in-game day every 300 s since the Unix epoch), so it is the same for every player. Cosmetics (hat colours, rain jackets, bobber colours): a few are sold in a fourth shop tab, the special ones are unlocked by achievements (a legendary, a shiny, 25 night catches, 10 lobby catches, a won Taktdrill, a rebirth, level 10, 20 fish discovered). The wardrobe (click your angler or K) shows a preview, offers free skin-tone and hair/beard-colour swatches, puts on what you own and tells you how to get the rest. Saved as `cosm`, shown to others in a lobby; without a hat cosmetic the hat keeps its rebirth colour. In a lobby there is a chat (Enter/T or 💬): messages ride along with the next sync, show as speech bubbles above the angler and in a fading log; the server keeps the last 30 per lobby and stars out words from the name blocklist. Multiplayer lobbies (👥 or O): up to 8 anglers share a 4-letter code (or a `?lobby=CODE` link); everyone sees the others standing on the dock with name tags, a feed of catches (toasts for rare and better) and a lobby scoreboard of Taler fished since joining. There are no sockets: `api/lobby.js` (same Upstash Redis) is polled every 3 s while the tab is visible; players vanish after 20 s without a sync, are removed after 90 s and lobbies expire 2 h after the last activity. The current lobby is saved as `lobby` so a reload rejoins. Size classes: every catch rolls Mini (~12 %), Normal, Groß (~16 %), Kapital (~5 %, up to 1.15× the fish's max size) or Monster (~0.7 %, 1.15–1.35× max), which multiplies the value (×0.7 to ×4); the best class per fish is saved in the Fischdex (`dex[id].cls`) and Monster catches are counted (`monsters`). Weather is derived from the real clock like the time of day (a new weather every 300 s, i.e. every in-game day, picked by a hash of the slot number, so everyone shares it): sunny, rain (faster bites), fog (more rare/epic), thunderstorm (more legendaries, more tension, lightning and thunder) and a rainbow that can only follow rain (double shiny and mutation chance), wind (counts as a longer cast), snow (+25 % Taler, slower bites), heatwave (less tension, slower bites) and shooting stars (more legendaries and shinies, meteors at night); the weather pill in the top bar shows it and the forecast. Four weather fish (Pfützenspringer, Nebelgeist, Blitzwels, Regenbogenforelle) only bite in their weather; the Kaufland-Fisch (a shrink-wrapped fish in a tray with a price tag, no logo) is a rare catch at the dock. The second map, Hohe See (open sea), unlocks with the 3rd rebirth: the buoy next to Bealin takes you out onto his boat (the steering wheel takes you back; saved as `map`); every cast there counts as far out and it has its own fish (Hering, Makrele, Thunfisch, Schwertfisch, Seeteufel, Riesenkalmar, Sturmhai in thunderstorms, Flaschenpost with the odd Taler, the Schiff (the Fisch GIF turned 180°, `img/schiff.gif`, own page `seiten/schiff.html`), a few dock fish that also bite out there (`sea: true`: Fisch, Poisson, Fisch 2, Ölfisch, Zackenbarsch, Blobfisch, Absolute Odfisch), and two deep-sea fish: the user-supplied dancing Gangnam-Fisch (`img/gangnam-fish.gif`) and the Monster-Energy-Fisch, an original pixel drink can with fins and no brand logo that gives faster bites for 60 s after you catch it). The Odköder (rebirth 3, 1500 Taler), cast at night on the open sea, summons the Odfisch, a big silver pixel fish with a cat's head ("FIH!"): a 3-minute fight with a health bar where reeling inside the safe tension deals damage, phase 2 adds Taktdrill salvos and phase 3 is a rage with harder pulls; a snapped line stuns for 3 s. Winning lands the Odfisch (at least Kapital size), 10 Schuppen, Taler by damage share and the Odfisch hat. In a lobby everyone on the open sea fights the same Odfisch: `api/lobby.js` keeps a shared boss record (HP 1000 per angler on the sea) and adds each player's damage per sync (capped, `HINCRBY`); the summon is checked against the server's clock (night only). More endgame: the Tiefseerute (rebirth 4), the talents Maßband (better size classes) and Wetterfrosch (stronger weather bonuses), hat colours up to the 10th rebirth, new Fischdex goals (first Monster, all weather fish, all sea fish, Odfisch beaten), new cosmetics and two new leaderboards (Monster catches, Odfisch wins). More ways to spend Taler: kiosk snacks in a shop tab (coffee: faster bites, fish roll: +25 % Taler, clover cookie: more rare/epic/legendary bites, glitter lemonade: double shiny and mutation chance; 10 minutes each, stackable to 30, shown as a pill in the top bar, saved as `buffs`), luxury cosmetics from 10 000 to 100 000 Taler (the two hats glint), and a leaderboard of Taler spent in the shop (`spent`). More progression: Fisch-Meisterschaft (bronze, silver and gold medals per fish for many catches, thresholds by rarity; each medal gives XP and Schuppen, each gold fish a permanent +1 % Taler up to +40 %; saved as `dex[id].m`, older saves get their medals added once), the Rutenschmiede (forge the rod in hand up to +10 in the shop for growing Taler costs, +3 % reel and luck, −2 % tension per level; saved as `forge`, reset on rebirth), talents up to level 10 from the 5th rebirth plus two new ones (Nachtangler, Bossjäger), ranks by rebirths from Lehrling to Fischgott (shown in progress, lobby name tags via `rk` and the rebirth leaderboard) and new unlocks at rebirth 5 (Abgrundköder), 6 (Krakenrute), 8 (Seebären-Jacke) and 10 (Fischgott-Krone). Prestige (from the 7th rebirth) resets everything a rebirth does plus rebirths, rank, Schuppen, talents and the forge; each prestige gives a permanent +50 % Taler and +25 % XP, the slot cap stays at the top, and Perlen (3, plus 2 for every rebirth above 7) buy prestige talents that never reset: Perlenhaken (+15 % Taler/level), Perlenglück (+10 % luck/level), Schuppensegen (+20 % Schuppen/level), Frühreif (rebirth one level earlier, up to 5) and Seekarte (open sea and Odköder without rebirths). Saved as `prestige`, `pearls`, `pperks`; shown as ✦ before the rank (lobby field `pr`), with its own leaderboard (`prestige`) and cosmetics at 1, 2 and 3 prestiges (Perlenkrone, Perlen-Pose, Prestige-Mantel). World 2, the Glutgrotte (lava cave, `map: 'lava'`), opens after the 1st prestige: Glimm, an original pixel mole in a miner's helmet standing on a glowing rock in the water in front of the dock, takes you down when you click him (in the cave he and a ladder take you back up); it has a rock ceiling with stalactites, a lake of lava, no weather (its own neutral weather `glut`) and twelve fish of its own with original pixel sprites (`img/lava-*.png`) and pages (Schlackebrocken with melted-in Taler, Glutgrundel, Aschebarsch, Bimssteinfisch, Funkenforelle, Obsidianhecht, Schwefel-Kugelfisch, Magmakarpfen, Salamanderfisch at night, Kristallwels far out, the legendary Lavadrache and the Phönixfisch after 10 Magmakarpfen). Normal rods melt there: only the Lavaruten (Basalt-, Obsidian-, Magma- and Drachenknochenrute, `lava: true`, from prestige 1 or 2) fish in the cave, held in their own slot (`lavaRod`, reset on rebirth like the other rods). Plus the Glutköder (only works in the cave), a Fischdex goal for all cave fish and the Lavamantel. A bait hotbar (bottom left, keys 1–9) switches between owned baits while idle. The UI uses a game-style skin: self-hosted pixel font Silkscreen (`fonts/silkscreen-*.woff2`, SIL OFL, licence in `fonts/silkscreen-OFL.txt`) only for short display text (titles, rarity banner, hints); everything people read (buttons, toasts, speech bubbles, card and quest titles, numbers) uses Poppins and square pixel-bordered panels, buttons and meters. The game can be played in German or English: a DE/EN switch on the title screen and in the top bar stores the choice in `localStorage` (key `fischen-lang`) and reloads; English texts live in `js/fischen-i18n.js`, keyed by the German original (without a saved choice the browser language decides). Progress is saved only in the browser's `localStorage` (key `fischen-save-v1`), see `datenschutz.html` section 5.
