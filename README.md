# Fisch!

Static site, no build step. Deploy on Vercel: import this repo, Framework Preset "Other", leave build/output settings empty. `index.html` is served at `/`.

## Fischen!

`fischen.html` is a small browser fishing game (canvas + `fischen.js`/`fischen.css`). Every catch is one of the site's GIFs/PNGs (incl. cut-outs from the Spezifischfischer page, whose catches speak their line from `spezifischfischer.mp3`). Catches give XP toward angler levels, which unlock gear; XP bonuses add up (not multiply) for catch streaks, clean catches (tension under 70 %), mutations, night fishing and the first 10 catches each day, and lost fish, Fischdex rewards and quests give XP too; three rotating quests pay out Taler and XP; from level 12 you can rebirth for Schuppen (permanent talents) and a lasting +20 % Taler bonus, with new rods, bait and hat colours per rebirth. A separate "Wii" rarity (between rare and epic, light blue fanfare) holds six fish cut from a user-supplied image (`wii-*.png`), each with its own page (`wii-*.html`). The Wii-Köder bait (icon `wii-bait.png`, from level 3) makes Wii fish bite twice as often. About 1 % of catches are shiny (recoloured, sparkling, triple value) and are collected in the Shinydex, a second tab of the Fischdex with its own rewards. Menus are things on the dock instead of buttons: the slot machine, the book on the crate (Fischdex/Shinydex), the trophy on the kiosk roof (leaderboard), the notice board (progress) and the kiosk (shop); the old buttons stay hidden for keyboard and screen readers. Catches can come out mutated (giant, tiny, albino, glowing, shadowy, golden, rainbow) for more Taler, and Fischdex milestones pay out Taler, Schuppen and permanent bonuses. An opt-in leaderboard (🏆) sends nickname and scores to `api/leaderboard.js`, a Vercel Function backed by Upstash Redis (env `KV_REST_API_URL`/`KV_REST_API_TOKEN` or `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL`/`UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN`; optional `ADMIN_TOKEN` to delete entries via `DELETE /api/leaderboard {id, admin}`). A secret key (☁️) stores an optional cloud copy of the save via `api/save.js` (same Redis, keyed by the key's SHA-256, 180-day TTL) so it can be loaded on another device. The shop is run by Herr Zylinderwels, an original pixel-art catfish in a top hat at a kiosk on the dock (click it); the shop window shows `tophat-fish-fish.gif` and gives every rod and bait its own pixel icon. Three dirty fish (Stinkfisch, Schlammfisch, Ölfisch) are original pixel sprites with their own pages. User-supplied fish GIFs (Gesprächsfisch, Zackenbarsch, Beinfisch, Blobfisch) have their own pages too. A bait hotbar (bottom left, keys 1–9) switches between owned baits while idle. The UI uses a game-style skin: self-hosted pixel font Pixelify Sans (`pixelify-*.woff2`, SIL OFL, licence in `pixelify-OFL.txt`) and square pixel-bordered panels, buttons and meters. Progress is saved only in the browser's `localStorage` (key `fischen-save-v1`), see `datenschutz.html` section 5.
