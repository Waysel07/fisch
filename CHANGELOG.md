# Changelog

Player-facing updates of the game „Fischen!“ (fisch.ing), newest first. Generated from `PATCH_NOTES` in `js/fischen.js` with the English texts from `js/fischen-i18n.js`; the same notes appear in-game as the Steg-Zeitung. See `CLAUDE.md` for how to keep both in sync.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-13`

- 🛥️ New boat skin for the high seas: “David Hasselhoff” (30,000 coins in the shop under cosmetics, from 3 rebirths). Instead of Bealin’s boat, a giant pixel Hoff with a curly mane, sunglasses and red swim trunks floats on his back in the sea, and you fish from his belly. The wardrobe has a new “Boat” slot for it.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-12`

- ⏱ New: your play time. The top bar shows how long you have been fishing (tap or hover to see the current session too), and Progress lists it with your stats. It only counts while the game is open and visible. You can hide it from the top bar in the settings.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-11`

- 👹 New in the Ember Cave: lava creatures! Sometimes a creature bites instead of a fish – Ember Snail, Ash Crab, Slag Worm, Flame Jelly, Basalt Golem and the rare Ember Kraken, depending on the depth. Holding deals damage, let go while it rages, and beat it before the time runs out. Loot: ember stones, coins, XP and sometimes an ember heart.
- 🎯 New stat creature chance (base 5 %, at most 30 %): lava rods from the Obsidian rod on, the Ember bait, the new Lure bait (lava shop, from depth 2) and Glimm’s new workshop upgrade Lure Call raise it.
- 🐟 Three new lava fish with catch conditions: the Soot Eel only bites on Ember bait, the Midnight Spark only between 0:00 and 3:00, and the Crater Warden only shows up after 25 defeated creatures, on a Dragon bone rod or better.
- 📖 In the Ember Cave the Fishdex now also shows the lava creatures you have beaten.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-10`

- 📖 The Fishdex now has one page per area: Dock, High Seas and Ember Cave, each with its own counter on the tab. It opens where you are fishing right now. Fish that also bite on the high seas appear on both pages.
- 🔎 Search and filters in the Fishdex: search by name, show only missing, only caught or fish without a gold medal, and filter by rarity. Works in the Shinydex too.
- 🕘 New in Progress: the catch history with your last 20 catches – fish, size, mutation, shiny, location and how many coins it brought.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-9`

- 👑 The Odfish has a new look: a cat head with a crown, a scar over one eye, fangs, a spiky dorsal fin and battle scars – finally like a true ruler of the high seas.
- 🎬 The Odfish fight now has cutscenes: he rises with cinema bars, a quake and a title card, leaps out of the water at every phase change (with glowing eyes in the rage phase), and the fight ends with a victory or escape scene. He also flashes when hit and shows the damage as numbers.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-8`

- ⛏️ Ember stones and the cave depth are now reset on rebirth and prestige – you dig your way down again each time. Glimm’s workshop, his trust and his jobs stay.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-7`

- 🍀 Luck rebalanced: rod, bait and talents still give noticeably more rare fish, but no longer so many that legendaries become an everyday catch. On the high seas about 15–20 % of catches are now epic and 4–5 % legendary instead of up to 75 %.
- 🌋 In the Ember Cave every cast counts as far out, like on the high seas. More rare fish bite down there, and from depth 3 the cave pays more coins than the sea. The sea gives more XP instead.
- 💰 The late rods cost more so they are a goal again: Star rod 15,000, Deep-sea rod 40,000, Kraken rod 120,000 coins.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-6`

- 📜 The Steg-Zeitung now has an “Older issues” button at the bottom to read all earlier patch notes.
- 🎣 Lava rods are reset on rebirth and prestige again, like all other rods.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-5`

- 🖱️ Rhythm reel: left and right click can now be alternated like in osu! – every click counts, even while the other button is still held.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-4`

- 🎯 Rhythm reel like osu!: aim at the circle with the mouse and hit with a left or right click (or Z/X) – it only counts while the pointer is on the circle. On phones you tap the circles as before, and Space still works without aiming.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-3`

- 🏆 Leaderboard: “Rebirths” now shows your current rebirths and drops to 0 on prestige, just like in the game. New rebirths show up right away.
- ✦ Prestige pays off more: +100 % coins per prestige instead of +50 %, and 40 % of your rebirths keep counting as a legacy. Talents are still reset on prestige.
- 🎖️ Rank, Old Salt jacket and Fish God crown now count all rebirths ever – a prestige no longer takes them away. Lava rods stay through rebirth and prestige.
- ⚖️ Economy: coin boni add up within their group (path, talents, collection) instead of all multiplying each other. Bait, snacks and boxes get a little pricier with your coin bonus. New: forge up to +15, Sun Crown (500,000 coins) and Galaxy Coat (1 million coins).
- 🌀 Each rebirth now needs only 2 more levels instead of 3. Every 5 levels there is a milestone with Schuppen, every 10 levels a mystery box.
- 📅 New weekly goals: three bigger goals per week, the same for everyone, with Schuppen, pearls and ember stones. Progress now always shows what comes next.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-2`

- ⚖️ Balance: the Ember Cave pays fewer coins. Cave fish are now worth about half, the heat bonus is +20 %, each depth gives +10 %, Glimm’s trust +3 % per level and the Ember Net +5 % per level. Glimm’s jobs pay about half. Ember stones stay as they are.

## 04.11.2026 · `2026-11-04-1`

- 🌋 Glutflosse’s lava store is now its own stall down in the Ember Cave – no longer in the kiosk at the dock.
- 🎣 The basalt rod now costs 400 coins instead of ember stones, so you can get your first lava rod without having any ember stones yet.

- 🎩 New lava cosmetics for ember stones: Ember Bobber, Ash Jacket, Magma Helmet and the sparkling Obsidian Crown.

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-13`

- 🔥 New currency: Glutsteine (ember stones)! Every catch in the Ember Cave drops some – the rarer the fish, the more (legendaries 40, trophy and monster double, shinies triple).
- ⛏️ The Ember Cave now has 5 depths: Ember Chamber, Ash Hall, Magma Maw, Dragon Hoard and Phoenix Nest. Glimm digs deeper for ember stones – every depth brings new fish, a new lava rod and +20 % coins. At the very bottom waits the phoenix feather rod.
- 🔧 Glimm’s workshop: ember stones buy permanent cave upgrades – Ember Net (more coins), Heat Shield (less tension), Spark Light (more luck) and Ember Crystal (more ember stones).

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-12`

- ⛏️ Glimm’s jobs: in the cave Glimm always has three jobs of his own with big rewards. Epic catches even earn you a pearl.
- 🤝 Glimm’s trust: every three jobs done he trusts you more – each level gives a permanent +5 % coins in the cave, up to +50 %.

- 🐟 Twelve brand-new fish that only live there – from the Ember Goby to the legendary Lava Dragon. And whoever catches ten Magma Carp wakes the Phoenix Fish.
- 🎣 Normal rods melt in the lava: Herr Zylinderwels now sells lava rods (basalt, obsidian, magma and dragon bone rod). Plus the ember bait, a new Fishdex goal and the lava coat.
