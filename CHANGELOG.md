# Changelog

Player-facing updates of the game „Fischen!“ (fisch.ing), newest first. Generated from `PATCH_NOTES` in `js/fischen.js` with the English texts from `js/fischen-i18n.js`; the same notes appear in-game as the Steg-Zeitung. See `CLAUDE.md` for how to keep both in sync.

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-12`

- 💰 The Ember Cave now really pays: every cave fish is worth three times as much, and down there you get a +50 % heat bonus on every catch.
- ⛏️ Glimm’s jobs: in the cave Glimm always has three jobs of his own with big rewards. Epic catches even earn you a pearl.
- 🤝 Glimm’s trust: every three jobs done he trusts you more – each level gives a permanent +5 % coins in the cave, up to +50 %.

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-11`

- 🐉 Gyarados now bites after just 14.5 Karpador instead of 25.
- 🌋 New world: the Ember Cave! After your 1st prestige Glimm, an old mole in a miner’s helmet on the glowing rock in front of the dock, takes you down to a lake of lava.
- 🐟 Twelve brand-new fish that only live there – from the Ember Goby to the legendary Lava Dragon. And whoever catches ten Magma Carp wakes the Phoenix Fish.
- 🎣 Normal rods melt in the lava: Herr Zylinderwels now sells lava rods (basalt, obsidian, magma and dragon bone rod). Plus the ember bait, a new Fishdex goal and the lava coat.

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-10`

- ✦ Prestige is now available from the 7th rebirth. Every rebirth above that still gives 2 more pearls.

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-9`

- ✦ New: prestige from the 10th rebirth. It resets everything, including rebirths, rank, Schuppen and talents – in return every prestige gives a permanent +50 % coins and +25 % XP.
- 🦪 Pearl talents: spend pearls from prestige on Pearl Hook, Pearl Luck, Scale Blessing, Early Bloomer and the Sea Chart (high seas without rebirths). They stay forever.
- 👑 New cosmetics for prestige: Pearl Crown (1×), Pearl Bobber (2×) and Prestige Coat (3×). Plus a "Prestige" leaderboard and ✦ in front of your rank.
- ✏️ Names for the leaderboard and lobby can now be as short as one character.

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-8`

- ⛵ Old friends on the high seas: Fish, Poisson, Fish 2, Oil Fish, Grouper, Blobfish and the Absolute Odfisch (at night) now bite out on the open sea too.
- 🚢 New fish on the high seas: the Ship – the Fish, turned 180 degrees.
- 🌦️ More weather, more often: the weather now changes every in-game day (every 5 minutes) and the sun shines less often.
- 💨 New weather: wind (your bait drifts further out), ❄️ snow (+25 % coins, slower bites), 🔥 heatwave (less tension) and 🌠 shooting stars (more legendaries and shinies).

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-7`

- 🏅 Fish mastery: catch the same fish often enough for bronze, silver and gold. Every medal gives XP and Schuppen, every gold fish a permanent +1 % coins.
- ⚒️ Rod forge: the rod in your hand can be forged up to +10 in the shop.
- 🐚 From the 5th rebirth talents go up to level 10. New: Night Angler and Boss Hunter.
- 🎖️ Ranks from Apprentice to Fish God – in progress, in the lobby and on the leaderboard.
- 🔓 New behind rebirths: Abyss bait (5×), Kraken rod (6×), Old Salt jacket (8×) and Fish God crown (10×).

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-6`

- 🎰 Fish machine: higher stakes – up to 2,000 coins, more with every rebirth (up to 50,000). New ½ and ×2 buttons.
- 💰 Jackpot pot: 2 % of every stake goes into the pot. Three Odfische win it on top.
- 🥪 Kiosk snacks in the shop: coffee, fish roll, clover cookie and glitter lemonade, 10 minutes each.
- 💎 Luxury cosmetics: crystal bobber, captain's jacket, diamond hat and crown hat.
- 💸 New leaderboard: who has spent the most coins?

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-5`

- 📏 Size classes: every catch is Mini, Normal, Large, Trophy or Monster. Trophy and Monster grow past the usual maximum and are worth up to 4×.
- 🌦️ Weather: sun, rain, fog, thunderstorms and sometimes a rainbow – the same for everyone. Each weather has its bonus (see the weather pill at the top).
- 🐟 New weather fish: Puddle Hopper, Fog Ghost, Lightning Catfish and Rainbow Trout. Also on sale: the Kaufland Fish.
- ⛵ New map – High seas: from your 3rd rebirth Bealin takes you out (buoy by the dock). With its own fish, from herring to giant squid.
- ⚔️ Boss fight: at night on the high seas the Od lure calls the Odfish. Three phases, three minutes – in a lobby you all fight one shared health bar.
- 🕺 New deep-sea fish on the high seas: the Gangnam Fish and the Monster Energy Fish (faster bites for a minute after catching it).
- 🏆 More endgame: deep-sea rod, Tape measure and Weather frog talents, new hat colours up to the 10th rebirth, new Fishdex goals, cosmetics and leaderboards (monster catches, Odfish wins).

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-4`

- 🕒 Shared clock: day and night now run the same for everyone – so in a lobby you always fish at the same time of day.
- 🎩 Cosmetics: hats, rain jackets and bobbers – a few in the shop, the special ones only through achievements. Others in your lobby see your look.
- 👕 Wardrobe: click yourself (or K) to put on what you have – and see how to get the rest. You can also pick your skin tone and hair colour there for free.
- 💬 Lobby chat: in a lobby, press Enter or T to type. Messages appear as a speech bubble above the angler.

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-3`

- 👥 Multiplayer lobbies: create a lobby (👥 or O) and send friends the code or link. You see each other on the dock, your catches and who has fished the most coins since joining – up to 8 anglers.

## 03.11.2026 · `2026-11-03-2`

- 🎵 Rhythm reel: legendary fish sometimes dance on the line. Tap the circles on the beat (or Space, Z, X) when the ring reaches them – with enough accuracy the fish is yours.
- 📰 The Dock Gazette: after every update a new issue with everything new lies at the end of the dock (also via 📰 or N).
- 📖 First-catch animation: a new fish shows up as a silhouette first, then in colour – with a “New in the Fischdex!” stamp.
